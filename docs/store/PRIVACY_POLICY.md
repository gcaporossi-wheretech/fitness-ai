# Privacy Policy — FitnessAI

Last updated: April 2026

## Overview
FitnessAI ("we", "us", "our") operates the FitnessAI mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

## Data We Collect

### Account Data
- Email address (for authentication)
- Name (optional, for personalization)
- Age (optional, for workout plan optimization)

### Fitness Data
- Workout sessions (exercises, sets, reps, weights)
- Workout plans (created manually or by AI)
- Calendar events and schedule

### AI Processing Data
- Photos of gym equipment (for AI vision scan)
- Body photos (for AI coach plan generation)

**IMPORTANT**: All photos are processed in-memory only. We NEVER store, save, or retain your photos on our servers. Photos are sent to our AI provider for analysis and immediately discarded.

### Technical Data
- Device type and OS version
- App version
- Crash reports (anonymized)

## How We Use Your Data
- Provide the fitness tracking service
- Generate personalized workout plans via AI
- Recognize gym equipment from photos
- Calculate statistics and progress charts
- Improve the service quality

## Data Storage
- Account and fitness data stored on our servers (AWS, EU region)
- Local data stored on your device (offline mode)
- Database encrypted at rest
- All communications encrypted via TLS 1.2+

## Third-Party Services
- **Anthropic Claude API**: processes equipment and body photos for AI analysis. Photos are sent via encrypted connection and not retained by the provider per their data processing terms.
- **AWS**: infrastructure hosting

## Your Rights (GDPR)
- **Access**: Export all your data (JSON format) from Profile > Export Data
- **Rectification**: Update your profile information at any time
- **Erasure**: Delete your account and all data from Profile > Delete Account
- **Portability**: Export data in JSON and CSV formats

## Data Retention
- Active accounts: data retained while account is active
- Deleted accounts: all data permanently deleted within 24 hours
- Backups: purged within 30 days of account deletion

## Children's Privacy
FitnessAI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you via email or in-app notification.

## Contact
For privacy-related questions: privacy@fitnessai.app
